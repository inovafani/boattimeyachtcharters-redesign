Most of what can go wrong on the day of a cruise happens before anyone steps on board: a wrong turn,
a full car park, a guest who thought the boat left at half past. This guide covers the part on land,
so the part on the water starts on time.

Every Boat Time sailing leaves from the same place. Guests on the
[Luxury Scenic Sunset Cruise](/luxury-broadwater-cruise) and groups on a
[private yacht charter](/private-yacht-charter) both board at Marine Stadium Jetty & Pontoon.

## Where the pontoon is

Marine Stadium Jetty & Pontoon is at Doug Jennings Park, Main Beach, on the Gold Coast. It sits on
the Broadwater side of Main Beach, near the Spit, rather than on the surf beach.

From the main centres it is a short drive:

- About 10 minutes from Surfers Paradise
- About 15 minutes from Broadbeach

When you put it into a map app, search for Doug Jennings Park as well as the jetty name, and check
the pin lands at the park before you set off.

## Getting there

**By car.** There is free parking at the pontoon. Check the signs when you park. If you plan to leave
the car overnight, for example after an evening event, street parking is the safer choice so the car
is not towed.

**By maxi taxi.** For groups this is the most popular option and usually the most cost effective. It
also means nobody has to drive home after a celebration. Book the return ahead for the time the boat
comes back, because a group waiting at a park after dark is the one part of the night that is easy to
avoid.

**For a large group.** If you are organising a charter, send every guest the same three things: the
jetty name, "Doug Jennings Park, Main Beach", and the boarding time. One clear message saves a dozen
phone calls on the day.

## When to arrive

Arrive before boarding opens, not at departure time. A boat leaves on schedule, and the pontoon is
the one place where five minutes late means watching it go.

For the sunset cruise, departure is at 5pm, and boarding opens half an hour earlier. For a charter,
your boarding time is set when you book. Either way, check the time on your confirmation the day
before, and allow extra time if you are bringing children or a big group.

## What to bring

- Your booking confirmation, on your phone is fine
- A layer for the evening, because it is cooler on the water once the sun goes down
- Flat or low shoes: boarding is a step up from the pontoon and the decks move
- Sunscreen and a hat for a daytime charter
- Any motion sickness tablet, taken at least 30 minutes before departure if you are prone to it

Food and drink are served on board by Boat Time's catering partners and the two bars on each vessel.
If you are planning anything of your own, such as a cake for a celebration, ask when you book.

## Access and mobility

The boats are not currently wheelchair accessible. Boarding requires a step up from the marina
pontoon, and space on board can be limited. If a guest has limited mobility, talk to the team before
you book so you can plan together.

## Questions guests ask

### Where do Boat Time cruises leave from?

From Marine Stadium Jetty & Pontoon at Doug Jennings Park, Main Beach, Gold Coast. Every sailing,
cruise or charter, boards there.

### Is there parking at Marine Stadium Jetty?

Yes, there is free parking at the pontoon. Check the signs when you park, and use street parking if
you will leave the car overnight.

### How early should we arrive?

Before boarding opens. For the sunset cruise, boarding opens at 4:30pm for a 5pm departure. For a
charter, use the boarding time on your confirmation.

### What happens if the weather turns?

Cruises run in all weather unless conditions are unsafe. Both vessels have covered decks and indoor
space, so a passing shower does not end the outing.
